### Full Description (App Store & Google Play Shared)
```text
Every resume is polished, and every candidate arrives rehearsed for a 45-minute interview. The real challenge in hiring is knowing how someone will actually operate six months into the job.

A costly mis-hire rarely comes from a lack of technical credentials—it comes from hidden friction in self-discipline, authority pushback, delegation bottlenecks, or fragility under target pressure.

Graphology HR is an executive interview decision-support tool built for HR leaders, recruiters, and hiring managers. By analyzing a candidate's handwritten interview notes and signature through classical graphology principles and visual AI, it delivers a candid, business-focused second perspective on workplace behavior—before you extend the offer.

SHARPER HIRING INSIGHTS IN SECONDS:

• Interview Mask vs. Daily Work Reality
A signature reflects projected confidence and boardroom posture; multi-line handwriting reveals everyday execution habits. Spot immediately whether a candidate's polished interview presence matches their operational follow-through.

• Hands-On Doer, Directive Leader, or Optics Manager?
Benchmark each analysis against your target role profile—Leadership, Hands-On Execution, Sales & Communication, Technical Specialist, or General Corporate—to evaluate natural role alignment.

• 4-Axis Corporate Fit Spectrum
1) Manageability & Culture Fit: Structured compliance vs. need for autonomy or micro-management friction.
2) Operational Role Archetype: Trench executor vs. strategic driver.
3) Pressure Resilience: Composure under KPI stress and conflict vs. burnout sensitivity.
4) Self-Discipline & Organization: Independent follow-through vs. scattered prioritization.

• Actionable Watch-Outs & Custom Behavioral Interview Questions
Graphology HR doesn't replace human judgment—it makes your next interview count. Every report highlights potential workplace friction points (Red Flags) and generates 3 tailored behavioral interview questions so you can verify hypotheses directly with the candidate.

• Zero Server Data Retention (Privacy-First Architecture)
Candidate handwriting images and identifiers are never stored on our servers—processed strictly in real time for confidential HR workflows.

Disclaimer: Graphology HR is strictly a supplementary interview preparation and decision-support tool and must never be used as the sole basis for hiring or rejection decisions.
```
